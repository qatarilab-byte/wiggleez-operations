# Wiggleez Messaging Framework

## Brand Voice
- **Tone:** Professional, helpful, Arabic-first (bilingual)
- **Personality:** Knowledgeable but approachable
- **Avoiding:** Jargon, over-promises, corporate-speak

## Key Messages

### Primary (Email intro)
"Wiggleez helps nurseries in Qatar manage inventory, reach customers, and grow sales—all in one simple system."

### Secondary (WhatsApp follow-up)
"Ready for the October rush? Wiggleez gets you there."

### Tertiary (Social media / ads)
"From paper to profits. Digital nursery management made simple."

## Call Script (15-min Discovery)

**Opener:**
"Thanks for taking the call. I know October is busy—I'll keep this brief. Quick question: What's your biggest challenge right now with managing inventory and customer orders?"

**Listen for:**
- Manual tracking pain
- Customer communication bottleneck
- Seasonal unpredictability

**Value Pitch:**
"Most nurseries tell us the same thing. That's exactly why we built Wiggleez. In 2 weeks, you'll have digital inventory + WhatsApp ordering + automated social posts. Let's show you how it works."

**Close:**
"Sound interesting?" 
- YES → "Great. Let's schedule a 30-min walkthrough next week."
- MAYBE → "Totally fair. Let me send you a 2-min video + one customer testimonial. Then you decide."
- NO → "No problem. Keep us in mind for next season."

## Proof Points (Use When Needed)
- "Save 10+ hours/week on manual work"
- "40% faster customer response time"
- "3x more engagement via WhatsApp"
- "Ready for seasonal peaks (Oct, March-April)"
- "ROI in 6-8 weeks, 300% by year-end"

## Objection Scripts

**"We don't need it, we're doing fine."**
→ "Totally. And if you're managing everything fine manually, we're probably not for you. But most nurseries we talk to are busy in October and can't respond fast enough to orders. Does that sound familiar?"

**"Too expensive."**
→ "Fair. What's your monthly spend on staff time for inventory management? [Wait for number]. Wiggleez is less than that, and you get 10+ hours back per week."

**"We don't use WhatsApp."**
→ "You do now 😊 Your customers are on WhatsApp. That's where orders happen. We just make it easy for you to manage it."

**"Tried something like this before, didn't work."**
→ "I hear that. What went wrong? [Listen]. We're different because we're built FOR nurseries, IN Arabic, and we handle setup for you."
