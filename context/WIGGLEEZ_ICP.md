# ICP: Wiggleez Target Nurseries (Qatar & GCC)

## Profile
- **Company Type:** Plant nursery / garden center
- **Geography:** Qatar (primary), UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait
- **Size:** 50-500 plants in active rotation
- **Annual Revenue:** 500K-5M QAR
- **Monthly Budget:** 2,000-10,000 QAR for supplies/services

## Pain Points (Ranked by Priority)
1. **Manual inventory tracking** — No digital system, relies on paper/memory
2. **No online presence** — Customers can't find or order online
3. **Inefficient order management** — Walk-in sales only, no delivery system
4. **Social media visibility** — No consistent posting or marketing
5. **Seasonal cash flow** — Back-to-school (Sept-Oct) is peak, need quick visibility

## Buying Decision
- **Decision maker:** Owner or operations manager
- **Timeline:** Decision to purchase within 7-14 days after first contact
- **Budget approval:** Owner (cash flow dependent on season)
- **Procurement:** Direct purchase, not committee

## Seasonal Patterns
- **Peak demand:** September-October (back-to-school, families buying)
- **Secondary peak:** March-April (spring planting, Ramadan gifting)
- **Low season:** June-August (summer, heat, minimal purchasing)
- **Strategy:** Contact NOW (Sept 18) → Close in 7-14 days → Deploy for Oct peak

## Value Proposition
- Save 10+ hours/week on inventory management
- Increase online visibility (Google, WhatsApp)
- Capture seasonal peaks with marketing automation
- ROI: Break even in 6-8 weeks, 300% return by year-end

## Messaging Pillars
1. "Digital nursery management for Qatar's modern growers"
2. "From paper to profits: Automation that works in Arabic"
3. "Ready for the October rush? We are."

## Objection Handling
- "Too expensive?" → "Costs $X/month, saves you 10+ hours. That's $Y ROI."
- "Not tech-savvy?" → "We set it up. You just use it."
- "Need training?" → "Built-in support in Arabic. WhatsApp away."

- **Approach:** Lead with efficiency + growth narrative

### Segment B: Traditional Nurseries
- Minimal digital presence

- Need hand-holding
- **Approach:** Lead with simplicity + ROI proof

- 5+ locations

- Higher budget
- **Approach:** Enterprise pricing, white-label option
